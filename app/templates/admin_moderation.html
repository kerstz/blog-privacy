Moderation

Blocked images

The block list is built from your own decisions. When you flag an image in a conversation, its fingerprint is recorded here and the person who sent it is banned. Any later attempt to upload the same image is refused — including a re-encoded, resized or recompressed copy.

Flagged files are moved to instance/quarantine/, outside the web root, and no route serves them. They are not deleted: a report to the authorities assumes the evidence still exists. Do not open them.
